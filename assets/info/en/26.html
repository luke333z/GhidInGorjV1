Trovant Museum – A World of Living Stones
The Trovant Museum is located in Costești, Vâlcea County, and is dedicated to a unique geological wonder in the world: the trovants or "living stones". This fascinating tourist destination attracts visitors from all over the world, eager to discover how certain seemingly ordinary rocks can have amazing properties. The Trovant Museum is a place that houses impressive collections of such stones, offering a deeper understanding of them and the phenomenon that makes them so special.
What are Trovants?
Trovants are a rare geological form that can be found in certain areas of Romania, including near Costești. These are stones that, under certain conditions, seem to grow in size over time, having a specific "bulbous" or "tree branch" shape. The "growth" phenomenon of trovants is still an enigma for researchers, but it is believed that this process is caused by chemical reactions between the water that penetrates the stones and the minerals inside them.

These trovants are actually clusters of stones cemented by a hard substance, and their "growth" phenomenon is an optical illusion. According to geological theories, larger holes inside the stone can collect water, and during the evaporation process, limestone crystals form on the edges, giving the impression that the stone "grows". However, the phenomenon is difficult to fully explain, and this mystery adds to the charm of the place.
History and Discovery
Trovants were discovered as early as the 20th century, but their phenomenon became widely known to the public only in recent decades. In Costești, geological research began to study this phenomenon and identified an area with a large number of trovants. Today, the Trovant Museum is located right in the heart of this phenomenon, offering an exhibition dedicated to these mysterious stones.

In the effort to protect them, Romanian authorities have declared this area a protected geological site, and in 2004 the Trovant Museum was established to preserve and educate the public about this unique phenomenon.
Museum Collections and Exhibitions
The Trovant Museum in Costești hosts an impressive collection of stones and fossils, many of which are trovants. Visitors can explore different types of trovants, each with its unique shape and characteristics. The exhibitions are organized both outdoors, where large stones can be found, and inside the museum, where smaller or rare specimens can be admired.

The museum also provides detailed information about the geological phenomenon of trovants, explaining how they form, how they "grow" in size, and the scientific theories that try to explain this fascinating phenomenon. The museum guides are prepared to explain the geological process and answer visitors' questions, offering an educational perspective on this phenomenon.
Geological and Touristic Importance
The trovants in Costești are a rare geological phenomenon that attracts not only tourists but also researchers from around the world. In their effort to protect them, several studies and geological research have been organized to analyze the nature and evolution of this phenomenon. The Trovant Museum contributes to the public's education about the importance of protecting this rare phenomenon, as well as the geological and natural value of the place.

Visitors who come to the Trovant Museum will not only learn about the geology of this region but will also experience a unique experience, being surrounded by stones that, apparently, live and change over time. Additionally, the area around the museum has remarkable biodiversity, and many tourists choose to explore the surroundings to better understand the geological context of the region.
Local Traditions and Folklore
In the Costești area, people believe that trovants are good for bringing luck, and this is reflected in local traditions. At certain times of the year, locals organize events where they bring the stones to the village center, and these are used to attract prosperity and well-being to the community.

There are also popular customs involving trovants, being considered symbols of regeneration and new beginnings. In many of these customs, the stone is brought to the center of the house to protect the family from harm and to bring abundance.
