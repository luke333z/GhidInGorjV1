Erasmus+ Forest Runcu
The Erasmus Forest in Runcu commune, Gorj county, is a remarkable ecological and educational project, achieved through close collaboration between educational institutions, local authorities, and non-governmental organizations. In the spring of 2024, on Earth Day, approximately 10,000 saplings were planted in the Runcu area, giving birth to the Erasmus+ Gorj Forest. This action involved students from institutions such as "Tudor Vladimirescu" National College Târgu Jiu, "Constantin Brâncuși" Technological High School Peștișani, "General Constantin Șandru" Technological High School Bâlta, the Center for Inclusive Education Târgu Jiu, and "Constantin Brâncuși" University Târgu Jiu. The initiative was supported by the National Agency for Community Programs and Training in the Field of Education (ANPCDEFP) and the Gorj County School Inspectorate, aiming to protect the environment and combat climate change by actively involving the local community and youth in environmental actions.
