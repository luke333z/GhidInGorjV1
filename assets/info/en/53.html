The Legend of Peștera Muierilor
It is said that in times of war, when the Turks attacked the villages in the area in search of loot and captives, the inhabitants of Baia de Fier had to find a way to save their families. The men, away at battle, were not there to defend them, and the village seemed unprotected.

Faced with this danger, the women and children fled to the cave, hoping to hide from the invaders. However, hiding was not enough – they had to make the Turks believe that the village was well defended. Thus, the women climbed to the mountain peaks and lit several fires, creating the illusion of a large number of warriors ready to defend the territory.

The Turks, seeing the lights of the fires and believing that a tough battle awaited them, decided to retreat, leaving the village untouched. The women and children remained hidden for days, enduring the cold and hunger, but managing to escape unharmed thanks to their cleverness.
