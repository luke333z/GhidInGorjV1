The Legend of the Trovants
Trovants, these mysterious stones that seem to grow and come to life, have sparked numerous stories and beliefs among the locals over time.

One of the most well-known legends says that the trovants are protectors of the land, left by ancestors to guard sacred places. It is believed that they were shaped by unknown forces and endowed with magical powers, having the ability to absorb the earth's energy and release it in times of need.

Another popular belief states that the trovants are symbols of immortality, being linked to the cycle of regeneration and eternal life. It is said that, on clear nights, the stones slightly change their shape or position, as if they have a life of their own. Locals say that those who touch a trovant with a pure heart will receive protection and luck for life.

Some elders say that the trovants hide ancient energies within them and that, in ancient times, the Dacians worshipped these stones as sacred beings. The legend also says that when someone tries to destroy a trovant or take it from its native place, nature retaliates with torrential rains or inexplicable events.
