Spring Fair – Tismana
The Spring Fair in Tismana marks the beginning of the spring season in the submontane area. The event combines agricultural tradition with local crafts, revives old customs, and brings the community together.
What does the fair offer?
Exhibitions of local products: From fresh vegetables and fruits to handcrafted items, the fair showcases the richness of traditional tastes and crafts.

Workshops and demonstrations: Visitors can learn authentic recipes and participate in demonstrations of traditional crafts, such as weaving, pottery, or wood carving.

Interactive activities: Games and contests for children and adults, aimed at reminding participants of spring customs and rituals.
