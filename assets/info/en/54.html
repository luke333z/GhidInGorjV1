The Saint Elijah Fair in Polovragi
This traditional fair takes place annually in the commune of Polovragi, Gorj County, between July 13–20. The event, also known as the "Nedeia of Polovragi," has deep historical roots, being documented as far back as the 17th century.
What does the fair offer?
Exhibitions and crafts: Merchants and craftsmen from all over the country showcase unique handmade products, traditional costumes, and accessories.

Traditional gastronomy: Visitors can enjoy authentic local dishes – from stuffed cabbage rolls, grilled sausages, and lamb stew to sweets and other traditional delicacies.

Folklore performances: Traditional music concerts and dances performed by renowned folklore ensembles contribute to the festive atmosphere.

Activities for children: Interactive games and creative workshops engage children in local traditions.

Pilgrimage and tourist trails: The event offers the opportunity to visit nearby attractions such as Polovragi Monastery, Polovragi Cave, and Olteț Gorges, completing the cultural and natural experience.

Schedule:

July 13: Official opening and inauguration of the fair.

July 19-20: Days dedicated to folklore performances and traditional contests (e.g., parade of traditional costumes and songs).

This fair is a true celebration of Romanian traditions, where the community gathers to celebrate local culture with family and friends.
