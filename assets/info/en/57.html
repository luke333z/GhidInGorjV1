Maria Apostol Folklore Festival – Runcu, Gorj
This event brings together young talents and established artists from rural areas, promoting traditional music and performance through singing and traditional instrument competitions.
What does the fair offer?
Interpretation contests: Participants compete in performing traditional songs, being evaluated by the jury for technique and authenticity.

Folklore shows: The event includes music and dance performances, highlighting the beauty and diversity of Gorj folklore.

Interactive workshops: Visitors can participate in workshops dedicated to learning traditional songs and instruments.
