Barza Airfield Târgu Jiu – A Center of Aviation in Oltenia
Barza Airfield is one of the important destinations for aviation enthusiasts in Gorj County, located approximately 10 kilometers from the city of Târgu Jiu. With an interesting history and a significant role in the development of local aviation, the airfield is an ideal place for flight enthusiasts and a reference point for aeronautical activities in the region.
History of Barza Airfield
Barza Airfield was established in the 1980s, initially aimed at supporting sports flying activities and pilot training. Over the decades, the airfield has become an important place for the development of recreational aviation in Oltenia, hosting numerous large-scale aeronautical events and competitions.

Over time, Barza has become a strategic location for training new pilots, as well as for leisure flying and aerial tourism activities. Over the years, the airfield has been modernized and expanded, equipped with modern facilities that allow a wide range of aeronautical activities.
Facilities and Activities at Barza Airfield
Barza Airfield has a well-maintained runway that can support both small aircraft and training planes. Additionally, the airfield is equipped with hangars for aircraft storage and facilities for pilot training, including classrooms and training areas.

One of the main attractions of the airfield is the opportunity to experience leisure flights or learn to fly. At Barza, beginner pilots can take pilot courses, and experienced ones can participate in training flights or even flight competitions. The airfield is also an ideal place for organizing aeronautical events, such as air shows, aviation promotion events, and exhibitions.
Connection with Local Aviation and Community
Barza Airfield is more than just a flying place – it is an aviation center in Gorj County, being a meeting point for aviation enthusiasts and those who want to learn more about the aviation world. The airfield also plays an important role in educating young talents in the aviation field, providing them with access to specialized courses and training opportunities in a professional environment.

Additionally, Barza Airfield is an important recreational place for locals and visitors, having a community of enthusiasts who frequently gather to participate in various events. Every year, the airfield hosts flight competitions, thus demonstrating its importance in local culture and promoting aviation in Oltenia.
Touristic Importance of Barza Airfield
Although Barza Airfield is mainly known for its aeronautical activities, it has also become a tourist attraction for those who want to experience leisure flights or admire aircraft in action. Offering the possibility to fly over the mountainous landscape of Gorj, the airfield is an ideal destination for thrill-seekers and those who want to explore the county from a different perspective.

Visitors who arrive at Barza have the opportunity to participate in airplane flights, learn about flying techniques, or simply attend various aviation events and demonstrations. Additionally, the airfield is an attraction point for aerial photography enthusiasts, offering spectacular views of the region around Târgu Jiu.
