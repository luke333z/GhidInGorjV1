Legends about Polovragi Cave
The Legend of Zamolxis and Polovragi Cave
It is said that Zamolxis, the supreme god of the Dacians, was once a wise man who wanted to discover the mysteries of the universe and the afterlife. After traveling the world and learning from the greatest philosophers of the time, he retreated to Polovragi Cave, located at the foot of the Căpățânii Mountains, on the banks of the Olteț River. Here, in the heart of the mountain, Zamolxis found peace, meditating and receiving revelations about immortality and the connection between man and divinity.

It is also said that in this cave, during his retreat, Zamolxis communicated directly with the gods, and the place became sacred for the Dacians. His priests came here to receive teachings and to be initiated into spiritual mysteries. Legend has it that, after a while, Zamolxis left the world of mortals and became a god, living in a hidden realm where only the chosen ones could reach.

Some locals believe that the spirit of Zamolxis still watches over the cave and those who visit it. It is said that if you are attentive enough, you can feel the energy of the place or even hear whispers from the depths of the mountain, as a reminder of ancient times.
The Legend of the Monks
Legend has it that, in medieval times, monks from nearby monasteries used to come to the cave for prayer and meditation. They considered this place a hidden sanctuary, far from the temptations of the world, where they could get closer to God.

One of the most well-known legends speaks of an old monk, renowned for his wisdom and unwavering faith. On a quiet night, he entered the cave with the desire to find his inner peace and to pray uninterrupted until sunrise. Sitting on a rock, he raised his hands to the vault and recited prayers continuously until the darkness melted into the light of dawn.
